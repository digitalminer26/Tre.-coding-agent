A live holder's touchLock refresh changes mtime and defeats removal, so a live entry cannot be removed through that path. Residual: the check-then-unlink gap is not atomic; an entry replaced between the final readFileSync and unlinkSync can be unlinked, but a replacement can only occur by its own creator or by an adversary who already controls the registry, and the header's residual covers planted entries.
